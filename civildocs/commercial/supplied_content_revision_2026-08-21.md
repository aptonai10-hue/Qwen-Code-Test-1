# Supplied Content Revision and Integration

The supplied review correctly identified three high-impact pilot friction points: an inert-looking document register, disabled account controls that offered no useful action, and pricing language that could be read ambiguously. CivilDocs now addresses these points with an actionable M1–M6 document register, a concise pilot-status note in place of unavailable account controls, and unambiguous **per-user, per-month** pricing language.

The recommendation to replace affirmative Terms acceptance with passive microcopy has **not** been adopted. The current controlled pilot has no user accounts or server-side acceptance records, so the visible affirmative acceptance step remains necessary for the pilot’s stated Terms workflow. It has instead been made smaller, clearer and directly connected to the document routes: after acceptance, a tester selects the desired document from the register rather than taking an extra generic “test” step.

The application copy was proofread as part of this integration. The revised entry flow keeps the terms disclosure, local-draft limitation and professional-review safeguards while removing non-actionable account visuals.
