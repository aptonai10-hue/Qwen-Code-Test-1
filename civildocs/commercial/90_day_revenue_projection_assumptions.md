# CivilDocs 90-Day Revenue, Expense and Profit/Loss Projection — Base Case

**Prepared:** 20 August 2026  
**Currency:** ZMW  
**Scope:** A planning model for gross subscription billings, operating costs and cash operating profit/loss. It is not a tax calculation, audited financial statement or guarantee of commercial performance.

## Revenue Assumptions

The model uses the proposed commercial tiers: **ZMW 199/month** for a Founding Engineer during the first three paid months, **ZMW 299/month** for Professional, and **ZMW 1,199/month** for a five-user Firm plan. It assumes four of six invited pilot engineers convert in the first paid month, followed by modest new-seat acquisition and one Firm plan in month three.

| Driver | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Founding Engineer paid seats | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| Professional paid seats | 0 | 2 | 5 |
| Firm accounts | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| Gross subscription billings | ZMW 796.00 | ZMW 1,593.00 | ZMW 3,888.00 |

## Expense Assumptions

Every expense input is blue and commented in the workbook so it can be replaced with an invoice, contract or observed usage measure. Until real vendor data is available, the figures below are **management planning assumptions**, not sourced market prices.

| Expense driver | Base-case assumption | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| AI model usage | 25% of included generation capacity used | Variable cost is ZMW 2.50 per successful generation. |
| Payment processing | 3.5% of monthly collections | Variable cost. |
| Hosting and infrastructure | ZMW 250/month | Fixed operating expense. |
| Support and administration labour | ZMW 1,000/month | Fixed operating expense; founder compensation excluded. |
| Sales and customer acquisition | ZMW 600/month | Fixed operating expense. |
| Software and communications | ZMW 150/month | Fixed operating expense. |
| Legal and compliance setup | ZMW 1,500 in Month 1 | One-time setup budget for lawyer review and commercial documentation. |

## Base-Case Result

| Output | Base-case result |
|---|---:|
| 90-day gross subscription billings | ZMW 6,277.00 |
| 90-day variable expenses | ZMW 725.95 |
| 90-day fixed operating expenses | ZMW 7,500.00 |
| 90-day cash operating expenses | ZMW 8,225.95 |
| **90-day cash operating profit / (loss)** | **(ZMW 1,948.95)** |
| Month 3 cash operating profit | ZMW 1,451.92 |
| Month 3 exit monthly recurring revenue | ZMW 3,888.00 |

The base case reaches a positive monthly cash operating result in month three but retains a 90-day cash operating loss because the model includes the Month 1 legal/compliance setup and ongoing support, acquisition and infrastructure costs. This is normal for an early pilot-to-paid transition, but it means CivilDocs should not rely on the model as a break-even forecast until the blue expense inputs are replaced with actual cost and collection data.

> **Boundaries:** VAT, income tax, founder/executive compensation, capital expenditure, debt service, refunds, bad debt and working-capital timing are excluded. Monthly billings are assumed to be collected in the same month.
