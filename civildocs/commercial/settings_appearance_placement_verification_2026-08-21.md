# Settings Appearance Placement Verification

**Date:** 21 August 2026  
**Environment:** CivilDocs development preview

The pilot entry screen was opened with the previously saved dark appearance. The masthead now contains only the CivilDocs identity, market label and pilot marker; it no longer presents an appearance button.

The footer **Settings** control opened a dedicated modal panel. The panel clearly separates the saved browser-local **Appearance** preference from the unavailable account settings. It showed the currently persisted **Appearance: Dark** state and stated that preferences do not affect document content, calculation rules or other users. The next check verifies changing the setting and closing the panel.

The Appearance control switched from **Dark** to **Light** inside the settings panel, immediately changing the background and text palette. Closing the panel returned to the entry screen with the masthead still free of the appearance control. The interaction retained the existing browser-local preference mechanism and restored focus to the footer Settings trigger.
