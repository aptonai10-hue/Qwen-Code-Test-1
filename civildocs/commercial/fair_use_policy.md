# CivilDocs AI Generation Fair-Use Policy

**Version 1.0 — Draft for legal review**  
**Effective for paid plans only when account-level billing and usage metering are enabled**

## 1. Purpose and Definitions

This Fair-Use Policy forms part of the CivilDocs Terms and Conditions. Its purpose is to make the AI-generation allowance clear, keep the service available to legitimate professional users, and prevent a small number of users from exhausting shared model capacity.

> An **“AI Generation”** means one completed request that CivilDocs sends to its AI service to create a new document candidate for a CivilDocs module. A PDF export, a download, a user edit made after review, a client-side validation error, and a retry caused solely by a documented CivilDocs service failure do **not** count as an AI Generation.

Included allowances do not roll over. A purchased additional-capacity pack expires at the end of the calendar month in which it is purchased unless the purchase screen states otherwise. CivilDocs will show usage information before a paid overage is charged once billing and metering are enabled.

## 3. What CivilDocs Will and Will Not Count

CivilDocs will count a generation when the service returns a candidate response to the user or otherwise completes the upstream model request on the user’s behalf. CivilDocs will not count a request that is rejected before it reaches the AI service because required form information is missing, the user is offline, the service rejects a malformed request, or a CivilDocs technical failure occurs before a usable response is returned.

If a user submits materially revised project instructions after a usable response has been returned, that new request counts as another generation. If a user repeatedly submits the same request after receiving a usable result, CivilDocs may serve a cached result where available; a cached result does not consume another allowance.

## 4. Prohibited and Restricted Use

The customer must not use CivilDocs to send automated, scripted, bulk, load-testing, scraping, resale, credential-sharing, or otherwise abusive requests. The customer must not use the service to generate documents containing unlawful, deceptive, infringing, malicious, or unauthorised material, or to upload confidential project information without authority to do so.

The customer must not attempt to bypass a plan allowance, rate limit, payment control, technical safeguard, or access restriction. A named-user plan may be used only by the named individual. A Firm plan may be used only by the customer’s authorised internal personnel within the purchased seat allocation.

## 5. Service Protection and Enforcement

CivilDocs may apply reasonable request-rate limits, request-size limits, duplicate-request safeguards, temporary queueing, or suspension where needed to protect the service, prevent fraud, comply with law, or manage an upstream AI-provider incident. These controls may apply even where a customer has unused included allowance.

CivilDocs will use reasonable efforts to notify the customer of a material restriction or suspension and to explain the available next step. CivilDocs may suspend access immediately where it reasonably believes that a request threatens service security, is unlawful, risks disproportionate provider charges, or attempts to bypass usage controls.

## 6. Pilot Status and Technical Transparency

During the evaluation pilot, no subscription purchase is created merely by use of the pilot service. Pilot access may be paused, changed, or withdrawn. The per-plan monthly allowances above are a proposed paid-service policy and must **not** be described as technically enforced until CivilDocs has live named-user accounts, billing, and per-account usage measurement.

## 7. Professional Review Requirement

CivilDocs creates editable draft material. The customer remains responsible for checking quantities, rates, dates, calculations, contractual references, project facts, approvals, and legal/professional suitability before use. No AI Generation, regardless of plan allowance, replaces review and approval by a qualified engineer or other appropriately authorised professional.

## 8. Changes

CivilDocs may change future plan allowances or overage pricing by giving at least 30 days’ notice to paid customers, except where an immediate change is reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, or an upstream provider outage. A change will not reduce an already purchased additional-capacity pack during its stated validity period.
