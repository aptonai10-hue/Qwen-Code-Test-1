This is a privacy-preserving device-local pilot tracker. It records completed document generations in this browser only and resets for a new local calendar month. It is not an account-level billing meter and cannot enforce a subscription across browsers or devices.

How the allowance works

An AI generation is one completed request to create a new document candidate. Downloads, review edits, client-side validation errors and retries caused solely by a CivilDocs technical failure do not count. The device-local tracker warns at 24 of 30 completed generations and blocks new requests in this browser after 30 until the next local calendar month. Clearing browser data also clears this local record.

Paid plan billing, named-user accounts and account-level usage enforcement are not active in the current pilot. Future paid terms will state the billing period, renewal, cancellation route and any overage price before payment is accepted.
