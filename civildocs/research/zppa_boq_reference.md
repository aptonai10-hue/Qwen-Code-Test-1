# ZPPA BOQ Reference Findings

## Primary Reference

**Source:** Zambia Public Procurement Authority, *Standard Bidding Document for Procurement of Small Works* (official e-GP download, September 2018).

The foreword describes the document as a ZPPA-prepared standard bidding document for small works and states that it supports admeasurement contracts using unit prices or unit rates in a bill of quantities, as well as lump-sum contracts using activity schedules.

The document structure separates bidding procedures, employer requirements, and conditions/contract forms. Its table of contents identifies **Section IV — Bidding Forms** and **Section VI — Employer’s Requirements**. The procurement cover identifies the procurement title, issue date, ONB number, and employer.

## Module 1 Alignment Decisions

The CivilDocs Bid Package output will be structured as a **priced Bill of Quantities / Schedule of Prices** supporting document, not as a complete statutory bidding document. It will include a procurement-style cover block with procurement title, reference/ONB number placeholder, employer/client, site/location, issue date, bidder/prepared-by, currency, and validity note.

The BOQ table will use section headings and hierarchical item numbering, with the columns: **Item No.; Description of Works; Unit; Quantity; Unit Rate; Amount**. It will include explicit sections for preliminaries, measured works, and summary, followed by tender-price summary and tenderer confirmation fields.

## Regulatory Context

**Source:** Zambia Public Procurement Authority, *Public Procurement Regulations, 2022*. The official regulations require distinct scope-of-works and bills-of-quantities preparation for relevant works procurement arrangements. CivilDocs will preserve a separate scope/technical-proposal narrative alongside the priced BOQ.

## Public-Works BOQ Cross-Check

**Source:** Parliament of Zambia, *Bill of Quantities — Preliminary and General Item* (April 2018).

The published public-works BOQ uses a centred **BILL OF QUANTITIES** cover, followed by the proposed works title, project location/employer, document date, bill number, and named bill section. It uses hierarchical decimal numbering, for example **1.00 PRELIMINARY PARTICULARS** and **1.01 Description of Works**, and carries values forward using a **To Collection** line denominated in **K**. These conventions support CivilDocs using a cover block, bill/section numbering, numbered headings, subtotals by section, a carried summary, and a final tender-price total.

## Implementation Boundary

CivilDocs produces a preparation aid and must retain a disclaimer that the output requires tender-document, employer, quantity-surveyor, and supplier-quotation review before submission.
