# CivilDocs Final Pre-Handoff Stress Execution Matrix

**Campaign status:** In progress  
**Test specification:** `research/civildocs_final_stress_test_suite_2026-08-19.md` (user supplied)  
**Execution rule:** A case is not passed until the stated observable result is captured from the published domain. Deterministic tests may support diagnosis but do not replace the required published-domain result.

## Evidence convention

Each completed row will record the exact form inputs, relevant review/PDF output values, production URL/version, and a defect classification where applicable. The applicable defect classes are: **D1** missing business-limit validation, **D2** duplicated figures from divergent sources, **D3** degraded line-item quality near boundaries, **D4** date drift/formatting, and **D5** semantic field mislabelling.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PENDING` | Not yet run in the campaign. |
| `PASS` | Published-domain result captured and checked against the specified expectation. |
| `FAIL` | Reproduced defect; record the D1–D5 category or a new category and the remediation. |
| `BLOCKED` | External service or production availability prevents a valid result; never treated as a pass. |
