# CivilDocs renewed Zambia real-world baseline — 2026-08-18

## Purpose and use boundary

This register distinguishes **official benchmarks and real-world control requirements** from tender prices, supplier quotations, contract determinations, and statutory approvals. CivilDocs must preserve the user's contract and supplier evidence rather than invent it. Any benchmark is a planning or reasonableness input only, and each final document remains subject to the signed contract, issued procurement documents, and authorised engineering or employer review.

## Live pricing rule

The app must not present a fixed material rate as today's market price unless it has a stated source and date. For the current test programme, a tester may use real contract, quotation, invoice, or ERB inputs. When a real supplier rate is unavailable, the record must say **benchmark / verify supplier quotation** rather than infer a current price.
