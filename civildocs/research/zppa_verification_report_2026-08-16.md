# ZPPA Tender-Format Verification Report

**Workstream status:** Complete  
**Review date:** 2026-08-16  
**Scope:** CivilDocs Module 1 — Bid Package Builder

## Finding

CivilDocs Module 1 is positioned as a **bidder-side preparation aid**. It does not create, amend, or replace an employer-issued tender document, Bid Data Sheet, specifications, drawings, conditions of contract, or addenda. This boundary is important because the official small-works template separates the complete procuring-entity document from the bidder’s submission materials.[1]

## Official cross-check and correction

| Official bidder-side requirement | CivilDocs correction | Verification outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Letter of Bid and tender particulars | Added bidder-completion guidance for procurement identifiers, validity, discount, representative, declarations, and signature fields. | The tool does not invent employer or BDS particulars. |
| Priced BOQ / Schedule of Prices | Retained numbered measured-work sections, hierarchical item numbering, quantity, rate, and amount fields; tender price now applies stated discount to measured works. | Fixed VAT and contingency lines are no longer represented as universally required employer-form components. |
| Technical Proposal headings | Added Personnel, Equipment, Site Organization, Method Statement, Mobilisation Schedule, Construction Schedule, and Others. | Headings match the published technical-proposal form structure.[1] |
| Qualification evidence | Added a bidder-package contents list and completion checklist for required evidence, security, JV documents where applicable, and authority to sign. | The output describes attachments to provide; it does not fabricate capacity, experience, personnel, equipment, or financial evidence. |
| Submission review | Added an ordered contents list, a Letter of Bid completion guide, and reliable PDF checklist markers. | The bidder is directed back to the issued tender and BDS for controlling requirements. |

> The completed package must still be checked against the employer’s issued invitation, Section IV forms, Bid Data Sheet, specifications, addenda, and tender-specific instructions. CivilDocs cannot determine those project-specific requirements from a general template.

## Functional validation

A realistic drainage-tender scenario was run through Gemini generation, flashcard review, and PDF export. The resulting bidder package retained the official-style section architecture, practical BOQ quantities, one non-duplicated honest-budget note where required, and the corrected attachment-checklist rendering.
